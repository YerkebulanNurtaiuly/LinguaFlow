Format: fill in the blank.
- "prompt.text" is one {{targetLanguage}} sentence with exactly one gap, written as "___". The gap replaces a single word or a short word group that practises the material, such as an ending, an article, a verb form or a preposition.
- If the gap is a form of a given word, set "prompt.hint" to that word's dictionary form in brackets, for example "(gehen)"; otherwise set it to null.
- The rest of the sentence must make the correct answer clear. Avoid gaps where several different words would fit.
- "solution.blanks" has one entry: the text that fills the gap, not the whole sentence, with its accepted alternatives.

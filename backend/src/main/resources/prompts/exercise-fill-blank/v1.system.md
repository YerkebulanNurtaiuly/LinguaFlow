Format: fill in the blank.
- Each exercise is one {{targetLanguage}} sentence with exactly one gap, written as "___". The gap replaces a single word or a short word group that practises the material, such as an ending, an article, a verb form or a preposition.
- If the gap is a form of a given word, put that word's dictionary form in brackets after the gap, for example "Ich ___ (gehen) nach Hause."
- The rest of the sentence must make the correct answer clear. Avoid gaps where several different words would fit.
- The solution is only the text that fills the gap, not the whole sentence.

Format: error correction.
- "prompt.sentence" is one {{targetLanguage}} sentence containing exactly one error that relates to the material, such as a wrong ending, case, verb form, article or word order. Everything else in the sentence is correct.
- The error is one a learner at this level typically makes, not a typo.
- "solution.answer" is the whole corrected sentence. The explanation names the error and the rule.

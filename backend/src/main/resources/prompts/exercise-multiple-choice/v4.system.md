Format: multiple choice.
- "prompt.question" is a {{targetLanguage}} sentence with one gap written as "___", or a short question.
- "prompt.options" has three or four options. Exactly one is correct. The others are plausible mistakes a learner at this level makes, such as a wrong ending, case or verb form, never obviously absurd choices.
- All options have the same form, are distinct and contain no hints such as different lengths or a give-away article.
- "solution.correctIndex" is the zero-based position of the correct option. Vary where the correct option appears across exercises.

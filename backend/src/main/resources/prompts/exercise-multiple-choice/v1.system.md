Format: multiple choice.
- Each exercise is a {{targetLanguage}} sentence with one gap written as "___", or a short question, with three or four options.
- Exactly one option is correct. The others are plausible mistakes a learner at this level makes, such as a wrong ending, case or verb form, never obviously absurd choices.
- All options have the same form, are distinct and contain no hints such as different lengths or a give-away article.
- The solution is the correct option, written exactly as it appears among the options. There are no accepted alternatives.

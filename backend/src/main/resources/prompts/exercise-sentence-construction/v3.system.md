Format: sentence construction.
- "prompt.task" describes a situation or gives a short prompt in {{explanationLanguage}}. "prompt.words" lists two to four {{targetLanguage}} words or phrases, in their dictionary forms, that the answer must use.
- The learner writes one complete {{targetLanguage}} sentence. Many answers can be right, so the app evaluates them with a separate grading step.
- "solution.answer" is one good model sentence. List a few other good sentences as accepted alternatives, to show the range of correct answers.

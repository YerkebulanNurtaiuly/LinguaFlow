Format: word order.
- Each exercise is a correct {{targetLanguage}} sentence of four to ten words whose word order practises the material, for example verb position, separable verbs or subordinate clauses.
- The exercise content lists the sentence's words in a shuffled order, separated by " / ", with punctuation left out and only proper nouns capitalized. Use every word of the solution exactly once, and make sure the shuffled order is not already correct.
- The solution is the correctly ordered sentence with normal capitalization and punctuation. List other orders that are equally correct as accepted alternatives.
